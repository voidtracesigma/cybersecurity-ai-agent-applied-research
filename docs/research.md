| Frontier AI&#x27;s Impact on the Cybersecurity Landscape | 2025 | Potter, Yujin and Guo, Wenbo and Wang, Zhun and Shi, Tianneng and Li, Hongwei and Zhang, Andy and Kelley, Patrick Gage and Thomas, Kurt and Song, Dawn | Comprehensive analysis (benchmarks, literature, experiments, expert survey) of how frontier AI (LLMs and agent systems) affects cybersecurity. It finds that AI’s current offensive capabilities often outpace its defensive use, highlights gaps in defensive tooling and benchmarking, reports expert views that attackers benefit more from AI (though the gap may narrow), and offers actionable recommendations to improve defense-focused benchmarks, secure AI deployment, and education. |

It categorizes agent capabilities (e.g., reconnaissance, reasoning, memory), examines attack techniques across network types, identifies limitations and defense challenges, and suggests future defensive research directions. |
